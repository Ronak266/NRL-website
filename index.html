Based on the NRL 2026 Game Manual, §§3–4 and rules G4.2, G4.3, G4.12–19, R9.1–4. The official manual and event updates take precedence over this training aid.

OPEN OFFICIAL GAME MANUAL ↗
01 / MATCH + FIELD

2:30. TWO SQUADS.

Two teams per squad compete on an approximately 12 × 12 ft field. Each squad has three High Poles, two Star Poles, two Apex Poles, a Source Zone, Drop Zone, Vault and Space Station.

Start with three field CORE per squad and 20 more in reserve. Each vault holds six squad-specific Golden CORE. Source staging may contain at most six squad CORE at once.

02 / CONTROL + PAYLOAD

TWO ELEMENTS. TWO MECHANISMS.

A robot may control at most two scoring elements, except inside its Drop Zone. This trainer counts PAYLOAD alongside held CORE.

Only your own team-numbered PAYLOAD may be carried. Use a separate Cargo Bay, never the CORE mechanism. PAYLOAD may touch the floor only in Source or Drop; deliver it fully inside your Drop Zone.

03 / STARBURST + STAR MAP

UNLOCK THE GOLD.

Scoring ten squad-colored CORE activates STARBURST automatically. With at least one delivered PAYLOAD, four scored squad CORE plus the Human Player's button activates it early.

Golden CORE may then leave your own Vault. Build the revealed bottom-to-top sequence of two Golden CORE and one squad CORE on each Star Pole to earn its STAR BONUS. A wrong order earns the individual piece points but no pattern bonus.

04 / LEVEL UP

FINAL 30 SECONDS.

Only in LEVEL UP may you Engage, Dock or Balance on your own Space Station. Engage means touching without full support; Dock means fully supported while tilted; Balance means fully supported while the station is level.

Only a robot stationed on its own Space Station may score squad CORE on its Apex Poles. Golden CORE cannot score on High or Apex Poles. Do not enter opponent Source or Vault areas, dock on their station, launch pieces or damage robots.
