We are Titan Forge, a six-member National Robotics League team. We are building for HYPERDRIVE, the 2026 game: a challenge built around collecting and scoring CORE rings, teamwork and end-game strategy.

From the first sketch to the next iteration, we bring our ideas to the table, learn from what fails, and keep moving together.
