TITAN FORGE WEBSITE - HOW TO VIEW IT

EASIEST: double-click Titan-Forge-Preview.html.
This single file includes the 3D robot, video, poster and all website code.
Open it in Chrome or Edge. It does not need a server or Python.

FULL SOURCE: right-click the ZIP > Extract All, then open index.html.
Keep index.html and the entire assets folder together. Do not open index.html
from inside the ZIP viewer and do not move it away from the assets folder.

WHAT TO TRY
1. The robot rotates in 3D on the home screen. Drag it to inspect it.
2. Explore in 3D takes you to scroll assembly, rotate, explode and wireframe.
3. Watch the Film takes you to a real 12-second video. Click WATCH SIMULATION.
4. Choose Assemble, Inspect or Activate to seek to a chapter.
5. Team profiles open when clicked. Photos and introductions await your content.

HOSTING
GitHub repository > Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > / (root).
Save, wait for deployment, then use the Visit Site link.
